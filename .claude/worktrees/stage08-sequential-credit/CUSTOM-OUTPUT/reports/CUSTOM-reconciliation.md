Generated by `efficientroute custom-report`. Every number is read from
`data/derived/CUSTOM-reconciliation.json`.

The customer layer carries no name, class or `oneway` — only an `Id` of zero
and a stale `Length` — so nothing can be joined on. Streets are matched by
**geometric proximity within 15 m**, and measured as
**length**, not as features: 3,368 customer parts against a directed arc table
is not a comparison, and a street half-present should come out half-matched
rather than being forced into a yes or a no.
