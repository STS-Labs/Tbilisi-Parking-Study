## 1. What was wrong with the sizing

Stage 4 sizes a day as `capture x overhead + legs + break`
(`zoning.zone_durations`), so `overhead` is the **intra-zone** multiplier and
the depot legs are a separate term beside it. The value it was given,
`ZONE_OVERHEAD_MEASURED = 2.55`, was taken from
`route_h / capture_h` — and `route_h` already contains both depot legs. Every
zone was therefore charged for its legs twice: once inside the multiplier and
once again in the `legs` term.

The distribution the **adopted** plan itself achieves — note this is the
outcome, not the input: the value Stage 4 was sized on is a quantile of the
*previous* run's distribution, which is why it does not equal any column here.

| Basis | p50 | p75 | p90 | p95 | max |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| `(route_h - legs) / capture_h` — what the model needs | 2.102 | 2.203 | 2.362 | 2.775 | 3.155 |

Sized on **2.2598** — `Stage 5 measurement, intra-zone p75 of 49 solved days (pinned by Stage 4c so the adopted plan regenerates)`. Measured where the routes are solved
rather than retyped into `config`, which is how the wrong quantity got in.

### The sweep

`prompts/04c-rezone-on-measured-overhead.md` asks for p75-p90. p75 is the
winning end of that band, and p50 was tried past it to find the turning point:

| Sized on | Overhead | Zones | Splits | Days | Route h | Finish | Artifacts kept |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| p90 | 2.3942 | 47 | 2 | 49 | 323.9 | 2026-10-12 | yes |
| p75 **(adopted)** | 2.2598 | 43 | 4 | 47 | 321.9 | 2026-10-10 | yes |
| p50 | 2.1024 | 39 | 16 | 55 | 328.2 | 2026-10-18 | no |

Each step down trades about four zones for about two splits, netting two days
— until p50, where the trade inverts and splitting becomes the main repair
(16 of 39 zones). A split turns one oversized zone into two undersized ones, so
once the tail starts breaking, every zone saved costs more than a zone.

**Why the constant is pinned.** Stage 4 reads the quantile from
`05-routes.json`, which Stage 5 rewrites — so leaving it live means each run
sizes on whatever the last one produced, and the adopted plan stops being
reproducible. The loop does not reach a fixed point: this plan was sized on
2.2598 and its own p75 is 2.2030. It is pinned to full precision because the
zone count is knife-edge in it — rounding to 2.2598 moves the partition from
43 zones to 44.
